Autumn in veterinary medicine always brings a rush.

…Vaccine clinics fill up.  
…Wellness visits stack back-to-back.  
…End-of-year checkups pile onto already full schedules.

And while your appointments may end at 5:00, the paperwork often stretches long into the evening. For many veterinarians, that means staying late, catching up on SOAP notes, and losing precious personal time right when the season gets busiest.

**The Problem:**  
Documentation is one of the top drivers of fatigue and burnout. SOAP notes are essential, but they’re also repetitive, time-intensive, and prone to delays when you’re juggling a packed schedule. Add in the need to double-check drug dosages, review differentials, or confirm protocols, and suddenly an 8-hour day turns into 10 or 12.
