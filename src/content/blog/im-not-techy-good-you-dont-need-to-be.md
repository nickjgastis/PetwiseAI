If you’ve been avoiding AI tools because they seem complicated, confusing, or like one more thing to figure out, you’re not alone.

**PetWise** was built with exactly that in mind. You don’t need to be tech-savvy to use it. You don’t need to watch a training video or memorize commands, although we certainly do offer demos (just contact us here to book yours) if you prefer the human-to-human approach to learning.

You just open your browser, type like you normally would, and **PetWise** does the rest! Many vets begin their **PetWise** journey with **PetSOAP**, the AI-powered documentation tool. You simply type what you’d usually jot down, your notes, shorthand, impressions, and it turns that into a clean, well-structured SOAP note in seconds.

![](/blog/im-not-techy-good-you-dont-need-to-be/image-1.png)

What surprises most users is how natural it feels. There’s no “learning curve,” no tech setup, and no pressure to change how you work. You stay in control of your case, **PetWise** just helps you finish faster, with less effort.

It’s the same with **PetQuery**, the clinical question tool. You can ask it things like, “What’s the recommended dose for this med in cats?” or “What are the differentials for this presentation?” and get fast, trustworthy answers, all based on trusted veterinary references you’d recognize and likely already use each day.

![](/blog/im-not-techy-good-you-dont-need-to-be/image-2.jpg)

If you’ve been skeptical about AI or overwhelmed by the idea of trying something new, **PetWise** is a good place to start. It’s NOT built for tech lovers, it’s built for overworked vets who want to get through notes faster, answer questions quickly… and maybe even finish on time for once.

In fact, the story behind **PetWise** says a lot about its philosophy - “built by vets, for vets”. CEO and Chief Developer Nick Gastis created the platform alongside his father, Dr. Stacey Gastis, DVM, after seeing firsthand how much time was being lost to daily documentation. He didn’t just sympathize, he built a solution.
