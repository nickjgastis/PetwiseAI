Dental charting is the record most clinics finish last. The procedure is done, the patient is in recovery, and the chart is still a napkin sketch or a half-filled grid in the practice management system. PetWise now writes that record from the same dictation you already use for SOAP notes.

This is veterinary dental charting inside a veterinary AI scribe. You pick dog or cat, say what you found and what you did, and PetWise builds a Modified Triadan chart plus a written dental record you can paste into any PIMS.

## Why dental records get skipped

A full canine mouth is 42 teeth. A cat is 30. Charting each one by hand, then writing the procedure note, the extractions and the home care, takes longer than the interesting part of the dentistry.

Most veterinary dictation software stops at the SOAP note. Dental charting software usually wants you to click every tooth. Neither matches how a vet actually talks after a dental: "upper right fourth premolar, slab fracture, pulp exposed, extracted." A vet scribe should be able to hear that and chart it.

## What you dictate

Say it the way you would to a technician. Tooth names or Triadan numbers both work. "Upper right fourth premolar" and "one-oh-eight" both land on 108. "Left mandibular first molar" is 309. A range like "406 through 410" is expanded into each tooth.

You choose the species first. The canine dental chart and the feline dental chart are different mouths, and cats are missing teeth a dog has, so the chart is locked to the species you pick.

PetWise only charts teeth you mentioned. A normal mouth stays a normal mouth. It will not invent findings, doses or teeth to fill the page.

Findings it charts include:

- Missing teeth, and teeth extracted in this procedure
- Fractures, caries and tooth resorption
- Periodontal disease, pockets and furcation, with the stage in the note when you say it
- Mobility, with the grade
- Retained deciduous teeth
- Teeth you treated and kept, such as a bonded sealant or a root canal
- Teeth you want to watch

## The chart and the record stay together

You get two things from one dictation.

The chart is a full Modified Triadan diagram, color-coded by finding. You can zoom in and tap a tooth to correct the status or add a note. That change is written back into the record, so the picture and the text do not drift apart.

The written record is plain text, in the order a dental note should read:

- Dental procedure summary, including anesthesia and overall oral findings when you mentioned them
- Tooth findings, one line per tooth, in numeric order
- Radiographs, when you dictated them
- Treatment, including extractions, blocks and closure
- Home care and follow-up

Empty sections are left out. If you did not take radiographs, there is no radiographs section pretending you did.

## It lives with the rest of the visit

A dental is rarely the only record for that patient. In PetWise the dental sits in the same case as the SOAP note, the discharge summary and the client callback. Open the case later and every record from that visit is there.

## Questions vets ask

**Does it use the Modified Triadan system?**
Yes. Quadrant 1 is the right maxilla, 2 the left maxilla, 3 the left mandible, 4 the right mandible. Spoken tooth names are converted to those numbers.

**Does it work for cats and dogs?**
Yes. You pick canine or feline before you generate. Each chart only contains teeth that species has.

**Can I fix a tooth after it charts?**
Yes. Open the tooth on the chart, change the finding, and the written line updates with it.

**Will it add teeth I did not mention?**
No. Teeth you did not talk about are left off the chart and out of the findings.

**Does this replace my dental module?**
No. Copy the note into your PIMS, or print the PDF with the chart attached, and file it the way you already do.

**Is dental charting a separate product?**
No. Dental records are part of PetWise, next to SOAP notes, summaries and callback notes.
