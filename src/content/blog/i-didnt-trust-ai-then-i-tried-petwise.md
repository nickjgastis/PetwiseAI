Let’s face it, most veterinarians didn’t sign up to be tech testers.

So when AI started making headlines in the vet med world, plenty of us had the same thought: “*That’s not for me*.”

*“I have been reluctant to use AI for records because I like things written a certain way, and it can be hard to trust information generated from outside sources*.”

That changed when she tried **PetWise**, and discovered it wasn’t like any other AI tool she’d encountered.
