November always makes us pause. It’s the season for reflection, on the cases we’ve seen, the patients we’ve helped, and the changes happening across veterinary medicine.

At PetWise, we’re especially grateful for the veterinarians who’ve welcomed new technology into their workflow this year, not to replace their judgment, but to make their days a little easier.

**What We’ve Learned This Year**

In hundreds of one-on-one conversations, we’ve heard one theme again and again: time.

Clinics are looking for ways to save it, without sacrificing accuracy, compassion, or the human side of medicine.

That’s exactly why **PetWise** exists.

With PetSOAP, record-writing no longer has to mean hours after closing.

With PetQuery, teams can find trusted, clinical answers in seconds, not searches.

Together, they’re giving veterinary professionals something truly valuable: time back.

**Built for Vets. Shaped by Vets.**

Every improvement we’ve made this year has come directly from the people using PetWise every day.

From small workflow tweaks to new integrations, every update starts with a conversation.

It’s why Nick, our CEO and Chief Developer, still meets personally with clinics. Those chats are where the best ideas begin.

**Looking Ahead to 2026**

As we move into 2026, our focus is simple: keep building tools that feel human, smart technology that understands how real clinics actually work.

We’re here to make your records faster, your workflows smoother, and your evenings a little freer.

Here’s to more time, less typing, and a brighter 2026 for everyone in vet med.
