AI scribes went from a curiosity to a clinic staple in about two years. Now there are a lot of them, and most of the marketing sounds the same. "Save hours." "Write notes in seconds." "Built for vets."

So how do you actually tell them apart?

We built PetWise with a practicing veterinarian, and we have watched a lot of vets try different tools. Here is what really separates a good veterinary AI scribe from one you stop using after a week.

## 1. It writes a record, not a transcript

A transcript is a wall of text. A record is a structured SOAP note with a clear subjective, objective, assessment and plan.

If you still have to read the whole conversation and pull out the findings yourself, the tool hasn't saved you much. The best scribes turn messy, real exam room talk into a clean note you only need to review.

**Test it:** dictate a quick, unpolished summary of a real case. If the output reads like something you would sign, that's a good sign.

## 2. It understands veterinary language

Human medical scribes struggle with species, breeds, vet drug names and clinic shorthand. "DSH, MN, 4y, BCS 6/9, mild dental tartar, NSF otherwise" should come back as a correctly written physical exam, not a guess.

Look for a tool that was built for veterinary medicine from day one.

## 3. It fits how you actually work

Some days you want the scribe listening in the room. Other days you'd rather dictate between appointments or type a few findings at the end of the day. A good scribe handles all three.

Mobile matters too. If you can dictate on your phone and pick up the finished note on your desktop, you stop carrying records home.

## 4. It follows your format

Every vet and every clinic has a style. Templates let you save the sections and phrasing you prefer so every record comes out the way you would write it yourself.

If a tool only offers one fixed format, you will spend your saved time editing.

## 5. It works with your PIMS

You shouldn't have to switch practice management systems to use an AI scribe. Copy and paste into any PIMS, plus a clean PDF export, covers almost every clinic.

## 6. It does more than SOAP notes

The note is only part of the paperwork. Client callback notes, discharge instructions and plain language summaries for pet owners eat time too.

Having a clinical assistant in the same place helps as well. When you need a dose, a differential list or a quick refresher mid exam, you shouldn't have to open five tabs.

## 7. You can try it for real before you pay

A demo shows you the best case. A free trial shows you your actual Tuesday. Use any scribe on real appointments for a week before you commit.
