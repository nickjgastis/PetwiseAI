January is a busy month in every clinic. You return from the holidays ready for a fresh start, yet a backlog of cases and unfinished records often greets you before the day even begins. It’s an intense way to open the year.

This is why small workflow changes matter so much right now. One of the easiest ways to create more breathing room in 2026 is to make your documentation process more flexible, so you can capture notes the moment it works best for you.

**QuickSOAP Lets You Type or Talk Through Your Cases**

Real clinic life doesn’t flow in a straight line. Some cases are easiest to type. Others are best captured by speaking while you move between rooms. **QuickSOAP** was designed with this reality in mind.

You can:

- record your findings on your phone

- use dictation on your desktop

- type directly into the SOAP format

- switch between typing and speaking on the same case

However you choose to record, PetWise organizes everything into a clear, structured SOAP note that syncs instantly to your desktop for final review.

**A Tool That Moves With You**

Clinic days shift constantly. You may have a quiet moment to type in one hour and be racing between appointments the next. **QuickSOAP** adapts to those transitions so you’re capturing information in the way that feels natural in the moment.

It’s a simple support that makes a meaningful difference in how your day unfolds.
