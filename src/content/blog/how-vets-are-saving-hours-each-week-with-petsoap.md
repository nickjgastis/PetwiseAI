We all know the feeling: consults are finished, the waiting room is empty, but you’re still staring at a stack of unfinished records. It’s one of the biggest contributors to burnout in veterinary medicine, and one of the hardest habits to fix.

That’s why we built PetSOAP, the record-writing assistant built right into **PetWise**. PetSOAP helps you take the notes you already have, or even your PetQuery thread, and automatically turns them into a polished SOAP note.

No more reinventing the wheel for every case. No more staying late just to finish paperwork. With PetSOAP, you can get your records done in minutes.

**How PetSOAP Works**

1. Type or paste your consult notes, or pull in a PetQuery summary.

2. PetSOAP structures everything into SOAP format: Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan.

3. Review, edit, and finalize, all within your PetWise account.

**Why Vets Are Loving It**

- Saves 15–20 minutes per case (which adds up to hours each week).

- Ensures consistent, professional formatting across the team.

- Built on veterinary language and context, no generic templates.

- Easy to copy directly into your PIMS, discharge notes, or aftercare emails.

**PetSOAP + PetQuery: Smarter, Faster Together**

Here’s where things get powerful: PetSOAP and PetQuery work seamlessly together

Use PetQuery during your consult to get accurate, clinical-grade answers in seconds, whether it’s differential lists, dosing protocols, or client-friendly explanations.

When you’re done, send that same thread straight into PetSOAP, which organizes it into a professional SOAP record you can finalize in minutes.  
It’s like having a virtual vet tech who not only helps you think through the case, but also writes the first draft of your record.

If you’re ready to reclaim your evenings and make records less painful, PetWise is here to help.
