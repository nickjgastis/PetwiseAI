If you’re tired of patient records slowing you down, you’re in the right place. At **PetWise**, we’re on a mission to bring speed, clarity, and accuracy to veterinary medical records, all without dictation or copy-paste fatigue.

Whether you're an associate vet juggling appointments or a clinic owner managing teams, **PetWise** is designed to help you reclaim your time and focus on what matters most: your patients.

### What Is PetWise?

**PetWise** is your all-in-one AI-powered assistant for veterinary documentation and clinical support. It includes two core tools:

#### PetSOAP, Create SOAP Notes in Seconds

Tired of spending 15+ minutes writing detailed records? **PetSOAP** lets you generate complete, editable SOAP notes from just a few clicks.

**Here’s how it works:**

1. Fill out a diagnosis or exam finding
2. Our AI assistant builds a full SOAP in seconds
3. Review, tweak if needed, and send it to the chart, done.

Your first 10 days are **unlimited**, and our subscription plans stay unlimited after that.

#### PetQuery, Your Trusted AI Vet Assistant

Have a clinical question mid-appointment? **PetQuery** is your on-demand, evidence-based assistant that responds in seconds.

#### What it does:

- Answers veterinary-specific questions
- Sources info from trusted references like Merck and Blackwell’s
- Gives reliable protocols, treatments, and medication guidance

You’ll never guess what’s appropriate or Google for answers again.
